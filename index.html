Sociotechnical Security

Georgia Tech CS 8803/4803, Fall 2026

This course examines how security and safety break down at the intersection of technology, people, and society. We'll study how harms arise from the design of digital technologies and from how they're used in practice, and explore the landscape of technical defenses and interventions against those harms. The course combines reading and discussing published research in usable security and privacy and digital safety with completing an original research project. Course syllabus
