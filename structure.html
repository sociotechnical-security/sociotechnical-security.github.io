🚩 Course Structure

This is a discussion-oriented research course. For each class, students will read 1-2 research papers and submit a reading response. Students will lead two discussion sessions. Throughout the semester, students will complete a research project in groups of 1-3. They will submit a project proposal, a progress report, and then a final report on the last day of class. Groups will present their project in lieu of a final exam on the final day of class. Grading will be broken down as follows:

📚 Attendance and Participation (15%)

We will be discussing the assigned research papers each class period. Therefore, students are expected to attend each class session and contribute meaningfully to the discussion. Participation will be tracked by instructor. Students will be allowed to miss up to 2 class sessions without impacting their grade. If other class sessions must be missed, please contact the instructor ahead of the class session to make alternative arrangements.

✍️ Reading response (10%)

Reading response instructions

For each class period, students will submit a short reading response to prepare for that day's lecture/discussion. Reading responses will be due at 2:00 PM on the day of class. Because the reading response is meant to help students prepare for that day's class, no late reading responses will be accepted. The two lowest reading response grades will be dropped. Responses are graded for depth and thoughtfulness, and students offering their own independent judgment over precise summaries.

🧑‍🏫 Leading discussion (25%)

Leading discussion instructions · Discussion leading assignments

Students will lead the discussion on the assigned readings twice throughout the semester. For this, they will be expected to prepare a 15 minute presentation and 5 discussion questions. Students will be assigned presentation slots by week 2. They will be expected to share the slides and discussion questions with Veronica at least 3 business days in advance and iterate with her on developing the final slides before the presentation date. Students are expempt from submitting a reading response on the days they are leading discussion. Students will be evaluated on the fair representation of the points in the work, presentation style, and how well they lead discussion with their peers.

🔬 Course Project (50%)

Project overview

Students will complete a research project in an area of sociotechnical security and submit intermediate deliverables.

  • Project proposal. Due 9/16 at 2:00 PM. Project proposal instructions

    • This document will commit your group members and the general project direction you are going in. You will sign up for a meeting with the instructor after the project proposal is submitted.

  • Project progress report. Due 10/14 at 2:00 PM. Midpoint report instructions

    • This document describes your current status on the project. You will sign up for a meeting with the instructor after the progress report is submitted

  • Final Paper. Due 12/7 at 11:59 PM any where on earth.

    • The final report should be 5-6 pages and include: a clear problem statement with motivation for studying the problem, a related work survey, a description of your study approach, and your results. If you built a system, such as for measuring abuse in a sociotechnical system, you should include the process by which your system was built and tested when discussing your approach. Final reports should be submitted using the USENIX LaTex template

  • Final Presentation and report. Due 12/7.
